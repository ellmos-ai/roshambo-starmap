## Notes on the figures

- **The Salt Crane** (SCr) — A weathered crane that stands at the river-mouth, holding the dawn in one lifted claw.
- **The Obsidian Beacon** (OBc) — A beacon of black glass that burns without warmth, lit for ships that sank before it was built.
- **The Silver Bell** (SBl) — A small silver bell that rings only for those who have already lost their way, and is polite enough not to say so.
- **The Silver Loom** (Slm) — A second loom, woven of moonlight, that runs only while the tide is coming in.
- **The Broken Anvil** (Anv) — The anvil no smith still strikes, left cooling since the last of the great forges went quiet.
- **The Silver Veil** (SVl) — A veil hung between two stars; whoever sleeps beneath it wakes without dreams.
- **The Drowned Bell** (DBl) — A bell drowned when the inland sea rose and has rung only twice since, in dreams.
- **The Windlass** (Wla) — A winch for raising water from a well that goes deeper than any rope can reach.
- **The Amber Loom** (ALm) — Sailors who passed the southern reef speak of a loom that still weaves nets for itself.
- **The Crystal Compass** (CCp) — A compass cut from a single crystal; its needle points toward whatever the traveller most regrets leaving.
- **The Silver Spindle** (Spi) — A polished spindle that catches every wind without spinning; nothing it holds will unwind.
- **The Ferryman's Oar** (Oar) — The lone oar left leaning where the ferryman last set his passenger down.
- **The Silver Sundial** (Sdl) — A sundial of tarnished silver that keeps perfect time, though only at night and only for the dead.
- **The Ashen Kite** (Kit) — A kite of ash, held aloft by air that nobody now remembers how to taste.
- **The Obsidian Mirror** (Mir) — A mirror of volcanic glass that shows the sky as it will look one winter after the last observer is gone.
- **The Hollow Chalice** (Cha) — The chalice the parish drank from once a year, then forgot why.
- **The Quartz Comb** (Qco) — A comb of quartz teeth that hums faintly when the air is thick with fog.
- **The Glass Eel** (Gee) — A transparent eel that slips between the constellations of the long river.
- **The Pale Anchor** (Anc) — A pale anchor heaved from a harbour that has since moved inland by half a league.
- **The Quiet Harp** (Hrp) — A quiet harp played only by the wind, with strings no hand has ever plucked.
- **The Iron Tide** (ITd) — A tide of iron that comes in twice a century and leaves rust where it kissed the sand.
- **The Sunken Choir** (Cho) — A sunken choir of stones, still humming the half-chord that drowned them.
- **The Foundered Ark** (Fka) — An ark that foundered before the mast was set; its keel still drifts through southern seas.
- **The Last Cairn** (LCn) — A cairn raised by the last traveller to walk the southern road; no path returns to it.
- **The Barnacled Sextant** (Sxt) — A navigator's sextant dropped overboard and forgotten, now overgrown with barnacles that catch the southern light.
- **The Frost Astrolabe** (Ast) — An astrolabe rimed with permanent frost; it measures the altitude of stars that set before anyone thought to look.
- **The Lanternfish** (Ltf) — A deep-water fish that carries its own small lamp, swimming the dark between the southern figures.
- **The Coral Quiver** (Qvr) — A quiver made from reef-coral, said to hold the one arrow that can strike a star without dimming it.
- **The Sulfur Anchor** (SAn) — An anchor left to pickle in sulfur water, its chain running deep enough to hook the next iron tide before it turns.
